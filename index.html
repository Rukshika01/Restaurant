Our story

A neighborhood room with a seasonal point of view.

Luma was created for guests who love generous hospitality and food that celebrates where it came from. Our chefs build menus around peak produce, responsible seafood, and comforting techniques.

The room is modern and intimate, with a glowing bar, open hearth, and flexible seating for everyday dinners, celebrations, and private gatherings.
