About Luma

A modern Colombo dining room shaped by seasonal Sri Lankan ingredients, open-fire cooking, and gracious service.

Premium hospitality

A restaurant built for memorable evenings.

Luma was created for guests who love polished hospitality and food that celebrates where it came from. Our chefs build menus around peak produce, responsible seafood, fragrant spices, and comforting techniques.

The room is intimate and contemporary, with a glowing bar, open kitchen, and flexible seating for everyday dinners, celebrations, and private gatherings.
