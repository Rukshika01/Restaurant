About Luma

Our Story

A modern Colombo dining room shaped by Sri Lankan ingredients, open-fire craft, and generous hospitality.

How we began

Born from a love of food, light, and gathering.

Luma began as a fictional restaurant concept built around a simple idea: a meal should feel thoughtful from the first welcome to the final sip. Inspired by Colombo's coastal energy, Sri Lanka's spice heritage, and the warmth of family dining, Luma brings refined modern cooking into a relaxed, elegant setting.

Our kitchen celebrates carefully sourced produce, seafood, and aromatics while using contemporary techniques to create dishes that feel both familiar and new. Every detail, from the glow of the dining room to the pace of service, is designed to make guests feel considered, comfortable, and delighted.

Restaurant philosophy

Modern dining with Sri Lankan soul.

Our philosophy is rooted in quality ingredients, seasonal cooking, creative expression, and hospitality that feels personal.
